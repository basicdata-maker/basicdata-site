Our app

Customer Assets for JSM Portal

Jira Service Management has a powerful asset database — but your customers can't see it. Customer Assets for JSM Portal closes that gap with a secure self-service view embedded right in the customer portal: customers see, update and report exactly the devices and licenses assigned to them.

Setup guide & documentation →

🔒 Self-service, default-deny

Every query is scope-bound server-side to the signed-in customer. Runs entirely on Atlassian infrastructure — no data ever leaves your site.

🔁 Four asset processes

Report a problem, register an asset, request a change, or ask a question — direct or ticket-reviewed, configurable per object type, with the affected asset linked on every ticket.

🌍 Multilingual

English and German UI out of the box, plus translatable object-type and attribute names. Customers see everything in their Atlassian profile language.

🩺 Guided setup & health check

A 4-step wizard, contextual help with a data-flow diagram, and a one-click check that verifies your Jira asset field end to end.
