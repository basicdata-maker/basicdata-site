Our app

Customer Assets for the JSM Portal

Jira Service Management has a powerful asset database, but your customers can't see it. Customer Assets for the JSM Portal adds a secure self-service view to the customer portal. Customers see, update and report exactly the devices and licenses assigned to them.

Setup guide & documentation →  ·  Use cases: licenses, fleet, MSP & more →

See exactly what's yours

Every customer gets a "My Assets" view showing only the objects assigned to them. Every query is scoped server-side to the signed-in account: default-deny, even against a manipulated frontend.

From asset to ticket in one click

Report a problem, ask a question, request a change or register a new asset. Each ticket is created through your JSM request types with the affected asset linked automatically, so agents never have to ask which device.

Edits you control

Per object type, choose read-only, direct edit, or reviewed change requests. Low-risk fields update instantly; sensitive ones go through a ticket your team approves.

Ownership that fits your schema

Owner on the asset itself or on a linked record such as Employee -> User — both work, and the app suggests the right path. No restructuring of your CMDB required.

Looks right in every language

English and German UI, following each user's Atlassian profile language. Translate object-type and attribute names so "NB-HW-Pool" shows up as "Laptop".

Setup that explains itself

A 4-step wizard with health checks, a one-click proposal that scans your site and fills in the configuration, and a live preview of your own portal while you map owners.

How it works

Live in three steps

1

Connect your project and asset field

Pick your service desk, request types and the "Assets objects" field. A one-click check verifies the field end to end.

2

Map ownership

Choose the object types customers should see and confirm the suggested owner attribute, direct or via a linked record. A live preview shows real results as you go.

3

Shape the portal

Set card attributes, status colors, detail views, editability and translations per type. Optionally turn on the dashboard start view. Done — customers see "My Assets" in the help center.
