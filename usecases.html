Customer Assets for the JSM Portal

One pattern, many use cases

Customer Assets for the JSM Portal shows people exactly the things that belong to them, and lets them act on those things without filing a blank ticket.

The pattern: someone — an employee, customer, student or tenant — has assigned objects: devices, licenses, vehicles, keys, machines. They want four things: see what they have, request something new, change or return it, and report a problem with the right asset already attached. If your JSM Assets schema can model it, the portal can serve it. Same app, same four-step setup; only the schema changes.
Classic IT

Give every employee their own device list

I got a new laptop eight months ago and I honestly could not tell you its serial number. With “My Assets” I open the help center and see my laptop, monitor, phone and headset, each with its status. When the screen flickers, I report it from the device itself and IT knows exactly which machine I mean.

  • "My Assets" shows each employee exactly their own devices, nothing else
  • One-click problem reports with the asset linked automatically
  • Replacement and offboarding returns as asset-linked requests

Setup: object types like Laptops and Mobiles with a user owner attribute. Works out of the box with the setup wizard.

Cost saver

Let unused licenses cancel themselves

Our SaaS bill grew every quarter because nobody knew what they were paying for. Now everyone sees their own licenses and seats in the portal, with the renewal date right on the card. Giving one up is a single click that opens a reviewed change request, so procurement stays in control.

  • Everyone sees their assigned licenses, seats and renewal dates
  • Request an additional license via ticket
  • Cancel an unused license as a reviewed change request

Setup: a Licenses object type with owner and renewal date on the card. Use the change-request process for cancellations.

ESM / Facilities

Keys, cards and desks without the spreadsheet

When I lost my access card, I did not know who to email. Now I open my assets, tap the card, and report it lost; facilities deactivates it before lunch. Keys, parking spots and furniture follow the same flow, and move-out returns stop being guesswork.

  • Report a lost access card immediately, asset linked
  • Request a key, parking spot or desk setup via ticket
  • Track returns of everything handed out at move-out

Setup: a Facilities schema or object types next to your IT types, each with its own presentation and icons.

Fleet

Drivers who know their own car

I drive a company car and carry a fuel card and a charging card, and until now the lease end date lived in someone's spreadsheet. In the portal I see all three, with the lease end right on the vehicle card. A parking scrape becomes a damage report with the vehicle attached; no license-plate guessing on either side.

  • Report damage with the exact vehicle linked
  • Request a service or tire-change appointment
  • See lease-end dates and hand the car over on time

Setup: Vehicles with a driver owner attribute, a status attribute for availability, lease-end date on the card.

B2B / MSP

A customer portal your MSP clients will actually open

As an MSP client, I used to email “the server is down” and wait for the reply asking which server. Now I sign in to my provider's portal and see the servers, firewalls and workstations they manage for me, with status. An incident report starts from the device, so their team starts fixing instead of asking.

  • Clients see exactly their managed devices and contracts
  • Incident reports start from the specific device, asset linked
  • Order or cancel a service package via ticket

Setup today: per-person ownership, including via a linked contact object (Contact -> User). Organization-wide ownership is on our roadmap.

Rental

Rentals that come back on time

I rented an excavator, and the return date is on the card in my portal, not buried in a PDF. When the hydraulics started leaking, I reported it from the machine itself in under a minute. Extending the rental is a change request the depot approves with one click.

  • Renters see their current rentals with return dates
  • Report damage in seconds instead of paper forms
  • Request an extension or trigger the return as a reviewed request

Setup: rental objects with a renter owner attribute, rental-end attribute on the card, change requests for extensions.

Education

1:1 device programs without the paper chase

My daughter's school issued her a tablet, and all I signed was a PDF I never saw again. Now I sign in, see her assigned device and its condition, and report the cracked screen the evening it happens. Year-end collection becomes a list, not a treasure hunt.

  • Students or parents see the assigned device and its status
  • Report damage and request a loaner from the device page
  • End-of-year collection against a clear per-student record

Setup: Devices with a student owner attribute — also via a linked student record (Student -> User).

Healthcare

Equipment that cannot afford downtime

On our ward, a broken patient monitor used to mean a phone call and hope. Now the responsible nurse sees the ward's assigned devices with status at a glance and reports a defect with the exact device attached, so biomedical engineering can plan around maintenance obligations instead of reacting to surprises.

  • Responsible staff see their assigned devices and status
  • Report a defect with the exact device linked
  • Request replacements before a shortage becomes critical

Setup: equipment with an owner attribute per responsible person; linked-record ownership supported.
