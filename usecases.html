One pattern, many use cases

Customer Assets for the JSM Portal shows people exactly the things that belong to them — and lets them act on them. That pattern is not limited to IT hardware.

The pattern: someone (an employee, customer, student or tenant) has assigned objects (devices, licenses, vehicles, keys, machines). They want four things: see what they have, request something new, change or return it, and report a problem. If your JSM Assets schema can model it, the portal can serve it.
Classic IT

Employee equipment

Laptops, monitors, phones and accessories per employee.

  • "My Assets" shows each employee their devices
  • Report a defect with the asset linked automatically
  • Request replacement or return at offboarding
Setup: object types like Laptops and Mobiles with a user owner attribute. Works out of the box with the setup wizard.
Cost saver

Software licenses & SaaS seats

Unused licenses do not cancel themselves. Self-service does.

  • Everyone sees their assigned licenses and seats
  • Request an additional license via ticket
  • Cancel an unused license in one click — a reviewed change request
Setup: a Licenses object type with owner and renewal date. Use the change-request process for cancellations.
ESM

Facilities & office

Keys, access cards, furniture and parking spots are assets too.

  • Report a lost access card immediately, asset linked
  • Request a key or a desk setup
  • Return everything trackable at move-out
Setup: a Facilities schema next to your IT schema. The app supports multiple object types with their own presentation.
ESM

Fleet & company cars

Drivers see their vehicle, fuel card and charging card.

  • Report damage with the vehicle linked
  • Request a service appointment
  • Hand over the car at lease end
Setup: Vehicles with a driver owner attribute, status for availability, lease-end date on the card.
B2B

MSP & service providers

Show customers the servers, firewalls and workplaces you manage for them.

  • Customers see their managed devices and contracts
  • Report an incident on a specific device
  • Order or cancel a service package via ticket
Setup today: per-person ownership, including via a linked contact object (Contact -> User). Organization-wide ownership is on our roadmap.
Rental

Equipment rental

Machines, tools and event gear on loan — visible to the renter.

  • Renters see their current rentals with return dates
  • Report damage in seconds instead of paper forms
  • Request an extension or trigger the return
Setup: rental objects with renter owner, rental-end attribute on the card, change requests for extensions.
Education

School & campus devices

1:1 device programs without PDF damage forms.

  • Students (or parents) see the assigned device
  • Report damage, request a loaner
  • Collect everything at the end of the year
Setup: Devices with a student owner attribute — also via a linked student record (Student -> User).
Healthcare

Medical & care equipment

Wheelchairs, monitors and loaner devices per ward or patient.

  • Staff see the ward's devices and their status
  • Report a defect with maintenance obligations in mind
  • Request replacements before they become critical
Setup: equipment with an owner attribute per responsible person; linked-record ownership supported.
